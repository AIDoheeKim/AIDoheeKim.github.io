### About the Special Issue

This special issue of *Mathematics*, an open access journal published by MDPI, focuses on mathematical and computational methods integrating data-driven learning, physical knowledge, simulation, and optimization for complex engineering systems. Particular interest is given to methods that enable reliable AI and optimization under limited data, physical laws, operational constraints, uncertainty, and dynamic environments.

### Topics of Interest

- Physics-informed and structure-informed learning
- Mathematical modeling of dynamical systems
- Surrogate and reduced-order modeling
- Constrained, multi-objective, robust, and stochastic optimization
- Uncertainty quantification and optimal control
- Reinforcement learning and learning-based decision-making
- Digital twins and intelligent engineering systems
- Prediction, simulation, and optimization for energy, manufacturing, transportation, and infrastructure

Original research, methodological studies, mathematically grounded applications, and review articles are welcome.

### Guest Editors

- **Dr. Dohee Kim** — Department of Artificial Intelligence Engineering, Changwon National University, Republic of Korea
- **Dr. Kyung-Hun Shin** — Department of AI Electrical Engineering, Changwon National University, Republic of Korea
