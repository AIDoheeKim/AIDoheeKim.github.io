About

DOLab develops data-driven optimization methodologies to solve complex real-world problems.

We bridge theoretical foundations and practical applications by combining mathematical modeling with data-centric AI approaches.

Research Areas

Time Series Analysis

Modeling and forecasting sequential data for real-world applications.

Machine / Deep Learning

Developing intelligent models using data-driven learning methods.

Optimization

Solving complex decision-making problems with mathematical optimization.

Explainable AI

Making AI models interpretable, reliable, and transparent.

Multi-modal AI

Combining multiple types of data for advanced AI systems.
